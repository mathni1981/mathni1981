### [O-Live: AI market intelligence for olive oil buyers](https://github.com/ericarobeen/o-live)
*UC Berkeley MIDS capstone, 2025 · Team of five*

A multi-agent system that forecasts olive oil prices, explains what's driving them, and answers procurement questions in plain English. Built on AWS: a weekly forecasting model on SageMaker, a conversational agent on Claude through Bedrock that calls forecasting tools, and agents that monitor data quality and trigger retraining.

**My role:** overall architecture; identifying and integrating six public data sources (prices, currency, freight, fuel, tariffs, packaging costs); exploratory analysis; and the automated data pipeline, built as six AWS Glue jobs.

`AWS Bedrock` `Claude` `SageMaker` `Glue` `Lambda` `Python`

### [Reducing source bias in fake news detection](https://github.com/mathni1981/two-stage-fake-news-detection)
*UC Berkeley MIDS, Natural Language Processing, 2025 · Team of three*

Fake news detectors catch AI-written fakes far more reliably than human-written ones. We tested whether two-stage architectures, which separate "who wrote it" from "is it true," reduce that bias.

**My role:** built the hybrid model combining ModernBERT embeddings with stylometric features; designed and built the veracity-first two-stage model; tested generalization on a separate dataset (ISOT); wrote the paper's abstract, introduction and background.

`ModernBERT` `RoBERTa` `DeBERTa` `Hugging Face` `SHAP` `Python`
